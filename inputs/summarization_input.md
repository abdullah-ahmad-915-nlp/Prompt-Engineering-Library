Banks and financial institutions use artificial intelligence for fraud detection, risk assessment, algorithmic trading, and customer support. Despite its many advantages, the adoption of AI also presents several challenges. Data privacy is a major concern because AI systems often require access to large amounts of personal and organizational information. Ensuring fairness and transparency in AI decision-making remains a key challenge for researchers and developers.
